Privacy Policy for Wellora

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wellora ("Wellora", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Wellora application and related services.

Wellora is a study and daily wellness tracking application that allows users to record study time, sleep, water intake, and mood, and may provide AI-generated feedback based on information entered by the user.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Wellora, the application may process the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you create an account using email and password, your email address is processed by Firebase Authentication.
  • Google Sign-In information: If you sign in using Google, information provided by Google Authentication, such as your email address and basic account information, may be processed for authentication.
  • Wellness information: Information that you voluntarily enter into Wellora, including study hours, sleep duration, water intake, and mood.
  • App preferences: Certain preferences, such as theme and notification settings, may be stored locally on your device or browser.

2. How Your Information Is Stored

Wellness entries such as study, sleep, water, and mood information are stored locally in your browser using local storage. This information is not stored in a Wellora cloud database through the current version of the application.

Authentication information is handled by Firebase Authentication. Firebase may process information necessary to create, authenticate, and maintain your account.

3. AI-Generated Feedback

Wellora may provide AI-generated feedback based on the wellness information you enter.

When you request AI feedback, the relevant values entered for that request, including sleep, study time, water intake, and mood, are sent to our server endpoint hosted through Vercel. The server sends these values to Google's Gemini API to generate the requested feedback.

The Gemini API is used only to generate the requested feedback. Wellora does not use the AI feature to provide medical diagnosis or medical treatment.

4. Third-Party Services

Wellora uses third-party services to provide parts of its functionality. These services may process information as described in their respective privacy policies and terms.

  • Firebase Authentication: Used for account registration, login, and authentication.
  • Google Sign-In: Used as an authentication option when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Gemini: Used to generate AI-based feedback from wellness information submitted through the app.
  • Vercel: Used to host the application's web services and server-side API endpoint.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by Wellora may be used to:

  • Provide account authentication and access to the application.
  • Allow you to record and review your daily wellness information.
  • Provide AI-generated feedback when you request it.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of the application.
  • Protect the application against misuse and security issues.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by the third-party services described above when necessary to provide Wellora's functionality, such as authentication, hosting, and AI-generated feedback.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Wellora. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Wellness information stored locally by the application generally remains on the user's device or browser until it is deleted by the user or the relevant browser storage is cleared.

Authentication account information is handled through Firebase Authentication and may be retained according to Firebase's systems and applicable policies.

If you want to request deletion of information associated with your account, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

9. Children's Privacy

Wellora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law without appropriate consent.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a way that violates applicable law, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Medical Disclaimer

Wellora is a tracking and self-reflection application. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

AI-generated feedback is for general informational and self-reflection purposes only and should not be treated as professional medical advice.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
