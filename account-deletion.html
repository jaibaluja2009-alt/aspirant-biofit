Delete Your Sora Account

If you would like to delete your Sora account and request deletion of associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request using the contact method below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your Sora account and include a request to delete your account.

Email: appjai08@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your Sora account so that we can identify the account for which deletion is requested.

What Will Be Deleted

Upon processing a valid account deletion request, the Sora account associated with the request will be deleted from the authentication system used by the application.

Information stored locally on your device or browser, such as wellness entries and app preferences, can be removed by clearing the application's local data or browser storage.

Some information may be retained when required by law, for security purposes, or where retention is otherwise legally permitted.

Processing of Deletion Requests

We may need to verify that the request is associated with the account owner before processing an account deletion request.

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or how Sora handles your information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
